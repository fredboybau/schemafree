 CONTROL POINTS  —  cohort sources for the SchemaFree federation

 A control point is a fixed, externally verified reference mark. Each line
 below was resolved live before being entered into the register. Marks that
 could not be occupied (no reachable station) are recorded at the bottom and
 are NOT used by the loaders.

 native taxonomy). Two larger collections enter the unlabeled pretraining
 pool only and never carry labels.
